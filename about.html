Profile

I don’t stop at the mock-up.

I prototype in Claude Code, design in Figma, and stay with engineering until it’s live. Most of my work starts as something you can click — prototypes pressure-tested in a browser instead of argued over in a deck. That way of working lives or dies on getting intent across precisely, written and spoken.

I get stuck into the front end and build my own designs in HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Years on developer-focused products means I understand database structures, APIs and real engineering constraints — including pharmaceuticals, a niche, heavily regulated space where accuracy and compliance aren’t up for debate.

AI runs through the whole workflow, from research analysis to ideation and prototyping. But speed is only half of it. I look for the opportunity worth building, then see it through to shipped.
