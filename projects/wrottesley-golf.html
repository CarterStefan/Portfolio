Overview

The brief

A historic parkland club with a dated website and a pro shop that closed when the doors did. The new site had to welcome visitors, convert members and sell, all in the club’s voice.

We rebuilt the club’s online presence around its two audiences: golfers discovering the course for the first time, and members who use the site week in, week out. We then added a store so the pro shop trades around the clock.
