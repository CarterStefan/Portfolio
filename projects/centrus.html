The brief

Translational safety assessments pull evidence from many systems, formats and teams. Centrus brings that work into one place — a single platform where scientists gather evidence, assess risk and report with confidence.

I worked as the product designer across the platform, alongside toxicologists, study directors and data scientists — understanding how assessments really happen, then shaping workflows that let the science lead and the software follow.
