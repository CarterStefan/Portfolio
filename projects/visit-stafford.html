The brief

A town’s worth of attractions, events, history and places to eat — and no single place that held it all. Visit Stafford became the town’s front door.

The design challenge was breadth without clutter: residents planning a weekend, visitors passing through and organisers listing events all needed different things from the same site — mostly on their phones.
