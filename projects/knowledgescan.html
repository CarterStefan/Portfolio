The brief

Target safety assessments were an expert-led service: bespoke, thorough and slow to scale. KnowledgeScan productised that expertise into a platform for advanced, repeatable assessments.

The heart of the work was translation — sitting with the experts who authored assessments, decoding their judgement, and rebuilding it as workflows, evidence views and structured reports without flattening the science.
